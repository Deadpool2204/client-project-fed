Frequently Asked Questions

Quick, easy answers about shipping costs, package sizing, and delivery.

Couriers bill for whichever is bigger: the actual weight on the scale or the box space (volumetric weight). Large, lightweight packages take up valuable truck and airplane room. The box space weight is calculated as: (Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 5000.

With COD, the delivery courier collects payment in cash from your customer at their door, then safely transfers the money to your bank account. A small handling fee of ₹45 (or 2% of the collected amount) covers cash security and bank transfer processing.

Shipments are categorized into easy zones: Local (within the same city), Regional (within your state), Metro-to-Metro (major cities like Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru), Rest of India, and Special Areas (North-East, J&K, Andaman). Closer destinations are faster and cost less.

Our rates cover the full courier freight and transport to the destination country. Any local import duties or taxes charged by the destination country's government are settled by the receiver upon delivery.
